# All of Us Are Dead

> A South Korean zombie thriller about students fighting to survive during an outbreak at their high school.

## About the Series

*All of Us Are Dead* follows students at Hyosan High School after a zombie virus begins to spread. Trapped inside, they rely on courage and teamwork to survive.

## What Makes It Exciting

- The suspenseful survival story
- The students’ teamwork and friendships
- The difficult choices they face during the outbreak

**Genre:** Horror, thriller, and drama  
**Format:** Korean television series
